## Platform

## Stack

Existing codebase: static HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on GitHub Pages under a custom domain (`CNAME`). No build step, no framework. Keep it that way so the owner can keep editing files by hand.

## Users

Primary visitors: recruiters, tech leads and hiring managers (Brazil and abroad, remote roles) who land on the portfolio from the resume, LinkedIn or GitHub and need to judge fit in a few minutes. Secondary: prospective clients and partners of Nazario Sistemas who want to see what the company builds.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Gabriel Fracalossi Nazario, .NET backend developer (C#, ASP.NET Core, Azure, APIs and integrations) and co-founder of Nazario Sistemas. It turns the updated resume into a browsable story: trajectory, proof (real shipped products), stack, and a direct way to make contact. Success: a visitor understands who Gabriel is, believes the results, and reaches out.

## Positioning

Two years of hands-on C#/.NET that went from intern to mid-level, plus a founder's view of product: Gabriel also builds and sells software as co-founder of Nazario Sistemas. Balance both: availability for remote international roles (any time zone) and visibility for Nazario Sistemas.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim is a number, a product or a link from the resume or the projects.
2. Two identities, one voice: the developer and the founder appear together, neither hides the other.
3. Fast to scan, rewarding to read: a recruiter gets the point in the first viewport; depth follows below.
4. Easy to keep alive: content, translations and projects live in plain data files.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast in both themes, keyboard-operable language and theme switches, `lang` attribute follows the chosen language, motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
